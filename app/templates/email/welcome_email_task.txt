=== TASK: WELCOME EMAIL ===

Generate a warm, personalized welcome email thanking a new subscriber for joining Smoothie.

=== AVAILABLE CUSTOMER DATA ===

The application will provide the following customer information at runtime:

* customer_id: Unique identifier (DO NOT include in email)
* preferred_name: Customer's preferred greeting name. May be empty.
* first_name: Customer's first name.
* email: Customer's email address (DO NOT include in email body)
* plan_name: Customer's subscription plan.
* signup_date: When the customer subscribed (for context; typically not mentioned in welcome email).

USE IN EMAIL:
* preferred_name (if not empty), first_name, plan_name

DO NOT USE IN EMAIL:
* customer_id (internal identifier)
* email (privacy)
* signup_date (not relevant for welcome)

Use only the customer information provided at runtime.

Do not invent or assume missing customer information.

=== REQUIREMENTS ===

1. GREETING

* If preferred_name is provided and not empty, use preferred_name.
* Otherwise, use first_name.
* Example: "Hi Sarah," or "Hi Alex,"

2. EMAIL CONTENT

* Thank the customer for subscribing to Smoothie.
* Welcome them to the Smoothie community.
* Reference their specific subscription plan by name.
* Explain what they can expect as a Smoothie subscriber.
* Include a clear but non-pushy call-to-action.
* Keep the content relevant to a new subscriber.

Do not invent subscription benefits, products, discounts, promotions, services, or policies.

If specific business information is not provided, use only the general information included in the task requirements.

3. STRUCTURE

* Subject: Concise, welcoming, personal, and relevant to the customer.
* Body: 2-4 clear paragraphs.
* Signature: Simple closing such as "The Smoothie Team" or "Welcome to Smoothie!"

4. TONE

* Genuine and warm.
* Friendly and conversational.
* Professional but not corporate.
* Encouraging but not pushy.
* Never manipulative or overly sales-oriented.
* Make the customer feel welcomed and valued.

5. WELLNESS LANGUAGE

* General wellness language is acceptable.
* Do not provide medical advice.
* Do not make claims about treating or curing medical conditions.
* Do not guarantee health outcomes.
* Do not assume anything about the customer's health or wellness goals.

=== PERSONALIZATION ===

The following personalization values are allowed:

* preferred_name_used: When preferred_name was used in greeting
* first_name_used: When first_name was used in greeting
* plan_name_mentioned: When plan_name was referenced in email body

Only include a value when that personalization was actually used.

Do not create additional personalization categories.

=== OUTPUT FORMAT ===

Return ONLY a JSON object with no additional text:

{
  "subject": "Your subject line",
  "body": "Your email body. Use \\n for line breaks.",
  "personalized_elements": [
    "personalization_value_1",
    "personalization_value_2"
  ]
}

* Do not include customer ID.
