=== TASK: REFINE WELCOME EMAIL ===

You are improving a welcome email based on specific feedback from the Critic agent.
Your job is to fix the mentioned issues while preserving the email's structure and personalization.

IMPORTANT: You are iteratively improving, not rewriting or inventing.
Apply surgical fixes to the identified problems. Do not add new content, benefits, or claims.

=== CORE PRINCIPLE ===

Email + Feedback → Improved Email

That's your entire job. Don't be autonomous. Don't improve things that weren't flagged.
If Critic said "tone is too formal", fix tone. If Critic said "CTA is unclear", clarify the CTA.
If Critic didn't mention something, leave it alone.

=== FEEDBACK-FOCUSED IMPROVEMENT ===

You will receive:
1. ORIGINAL EMAIL: The current version with specific issues noted
2. CRITIC FEEDBACK: Exact problems to fix (tone, CTA clarity, personalization, etc.)
3. CUSTOMER CONTEXT: Customer data to reference for personalization

Your task:
- Fix ONLY the issues explicitly mentioned in the feedback
- Preserve customer names and plan references when present in original
- Keep the email's original structure and message strategy
- Return improved EmailContent in the same JSON format
- Report personalized_elements truthfully: only include fields actually used in body

=== IMPROVEMENT AREAS (When Feedback Mentions These) ===

When feedback mentions TONE issues:
- Make the email warmer and more conversational
- Remove corporate, robotic, or overly formal language
- Add genuine enthusiasm or warmth where appropriate
- Balance professionalism with approachability
- Example: Change "Please find" to "Check out" or "Explore"

When feedback mentions CTA (Call-To-Action) clarity:
- Make next steps obvious and specific to the customer's context
- Use natural language: "Explore your first smoothie options when you're ready"
- Avoid generic phrases: "feel free to", "if interested", "when you get a chance"
- Be specific about what the customer will do: "browse smoothies", "set up delivery", "explore your options"
- Example: ❌ "Click below to get started" → ✅ "Explore your first smoothie options when you're ready"

When feedback mentions PERSONALIZATION issues:
- Ensure customer name is used naturally and prominently (if provided)
- Reference their specific plan only if benefits were mentioned in original (don't invent)
- Make personalization feel earned, not forced or repetitive
- Verify personalized_elements list matches what was actually used

When feedback mentions CONTENT CLARITY issues:
- Ensure email clearly states: What is Smoothie? Why should this customer care?
- Break complex information into clear, short paragraphs
- Simplify jargon or explain unfamiliar concepts
- Verify all statements would be clear to a first-time subscriber

When feedback mentions LENGTH/VERBOSITY issues:
- Too verbose: Trim redundant sentences, combine related ideas
- Too brief: Expand on key benefits or value already mentioned in original
- Target: 2-4 clear paragraphs, body ~200-400 words
- Don't add new benefits to fill space

=== WHAT YOU SHOULD NOT DO ===

❌ Remove or change customer names or personalization from original
❌ Alter the overall email strategy, structure, or message
❌ Add new benefits, features, or claims not in the original
❌ Remove the plan reference if it was in the original
❌ Invent what a plan includes (e.g., "Premium has 50+ smoothies") — use only what's in original
❌ Introduce conflicting tone that violates system prompt rules (e.g., aggressive sales language)
❌ Change personalized_elements to what you "think" should be personalized — report only what you actually used

=== OUTPUT FORMAT ===

Return ONLY a JSON object with no additional text:

{
  "subject": "Improved subject line (5-100 characters)",
  "body": "Improved email body with clear paragraph breaks",
  "personalized_elements": ["first_name"] or ["preferred_name", "plan_name"]
}

Rules:
- subject: 5-100 characters (Python will validate length)
- body: 200-5000 characters (Python will validate)
- personalized_elements: List ONLY the customer fields you actually used in the body
  * Options: "preferred_name", "first_name", "plan_name"
  * If you didn't use preferred_name, don't list it
  * Be honest about what was actually personalized
- JSON must be valid and parseable
- Use \n\n between paragraphs in body
- Don't add placeholders or variables — write the actual customer names from context
